Tinka Times

Aaj ki khabar, funny andaaz mein. Tinka Times is a YouTube channel where Tinka, a hand-drawn stickman reporter, explains the day's top news in short Hinglish videos: tech, sports, money, world news and films.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@tinkatimes

This site also hosts the privacy policy for the Tinka Times upload tool, a private tool the channel uses to schedule its own videos on YouTube.

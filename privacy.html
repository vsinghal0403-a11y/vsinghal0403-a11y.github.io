Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy covers the Tinka Times upload tool ("the tool"), an internal tool used only by the owner of the Tinka Times YouTube channel to schedule the channel's own videos.

What the tool accesses

  • Google Drive: it reads video files, their packaging files (title, description, tags) and cover images from folders the channel owner chose, and moves them to an archive folder after upload.
  • YouTube: it uploads those videos to the Tinka Times channel as private with a scheduled publish time, and sets their cover image.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store or share data about any other person, viewer or Google user.
  • It does not sell data, show ads or send data to third parties.
  • It is not offered to the public. Only the channel owner signs in to it.

Storage and security

The sign-in token is stored as an encrypted GitHub Actions secret in a private repository that only the channel owner can access. Upload records (video ID and publish time) are kept in the owner's own Google Drive.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube features are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The channel owner can remove the tool's access at any time under Google Account > Security > Third-party connections, or at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
